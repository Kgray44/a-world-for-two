placement** of the held80-cell packet. It keeps all80 original cells unchanged
and adds supported carved knees, narrow flower ledges, a pilot shelf with mugs,
a shaped canopy return, two rooted flowers/shrubs and an actual repair anvil.

its failed support receipt remain preserved. No coastal fill is introduced.

the current world and all human changes. Native55 rooms, stock and every future
main-building floor remain protected. No new container, invented Items or extra
BE tags are added. The existing three waxed signs remain unchanged.
