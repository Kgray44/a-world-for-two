# Source-bound town LIFE pilot

Four reviewed, flat-floor sites use the physically verified Rose Reach source:
an unadopted cat, untamed dog, ordinary Ribbit in the music salon and one villager
beside a genuinely free cartographer workstation. All remain within the existing
single curated engine and original cohort namespace. No traits, profession,
inventory, taming, music role, ownership or artificial inactivity are assigned.

The saved fancy model beds have no HOME POIs. A separate two-cell ordinary blue
bed is proposed for unused ground-floor AIR in the chartmaker's rear nook,
preserving every old furnishing, BE and inventory. Its source does not establish
native HOME acceptance: the parent must verify/place it, verify the amended full
source, save, then rebind a complete habitat/census and inspect actual available
native HOME/job POIs. No stair approximation is used in the flat reviewed routes.

`prepare.py` writes original authority/site/preimage files only. The three pet /
music sites can be joined to the woodland cohort separately; the four-site file
is explicitly for use after the native HOME addition. It never creates or
installs executable requests and never spawns a resident. Native obstruction,
normal STRUCTURE spawn hooks, artifact/class pins, default traits, construction
approval, cleanup and actual player AI observations remain separate gates.
