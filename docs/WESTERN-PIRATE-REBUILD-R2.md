# Western pirate-town rebuild: first frontage

The earlier northern extensions failed the owner's artistic review: the roofs
were too bright and striped, houses stood in rigid rows, and the western land
remained too open. Their source and receipts remain historical evidence. The
extra twenty-house replication and the old north-town botanical pilots are held.

The reference is the original intimate pirate section: dark irregular timber,
muted plaster and weathered stone, real fancy glazing, bracket lamps, closely
facing upper floors and narrow crooked lanes. The original accepted section,
every current inventory, the owner's later changes and the western zipline
structures/flight space are preserved.

The new western measurement samples exact saved one-metre columns, with a
separate north/south flood and a bridge barrier. It identifies 11,571 northern
connected dry/retained columns within the studied western land. Retained roofs,
decks and missing cells do **not** confer building authority. The fifteen older
western houses contain 130 saved BE/model anchors whose contents remain unknown;
none may be silently erased or moved.

The first preview is deliberately only three original connected frontages:

| Building | Form and street relationship | Purposeful rooms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Bent Wick | Bent two-storey outline, small upper jetty, lower rear roof, broad casement and one-metre braced porch facing south | Oil/wick repair shop and a compact upstairs home |
| Widow Finch's Cooperage | Taller narrow mass set back two metres; door turns west, with a supported south bay and continuous charcoal roof | Cask workshop, family parlour and navigator's attic |
| The Ragged Sail | Low chamfered house opposite the alley; half-hipped weathered roof and one real glazed dormer | Canvas-cutting workroom and a reachable sleeping loft |

There are seven planned rooms, genuine stair flights, normal work stations,
storage, cupboards, beds, seating, books and trade objects. Shallow ceiling ribs,
window recesses/projections, small roof supports, corbels and braced entries add
physical depth. The two-wide bend wraps around an **existing** pottery barrow;
its post, planters, table, vessels and lamp remain in place. The preview does not
demolish any existing house or touch an existing BE.

The 28 October material relaxation permits intentional unlimited runs along one
axis, including coherent dark roof edges. A building still cannot have runs
longer than three along multiple axes for the same material ID. Different block
facings do not count as material changes. This design uses real roof-level
changes, a half hip and a dormer; no bright periodic bands enforce the rule.

The initial exact 575aff preimage contains 3,775 addressed cells over 249 main
dry/support columns. Where actual exposed gravel is one metre lower, a shallow
stone skirt/joist footing supports the floor; the beach is not flattened. Nine
old public paving cells change, each bound to the earlier exact gravel preimage.
All other existing house and BE cells, and the full zipline mask, are excluded.
The current stopped backup and a new native preimage remain mandatory before
execution; an old source receipt never authorizes overwriting later play.

`tools.artistic_west_r2_frontage` produces a pure source-review bundle or a
private proposal tied to a verified stopped artistic checkpoint. It makes no
world calls. The native sequence is: exact before/support verification, one
preview placement, ordinary neighbor settling, complete state/NBT verification,
then actual street/roof/interior screenshots and refinement. Source checks do
not certify beauty, native model scale, walkability or owner acceptance. No wider
replication is qualified until the actual first preview is reviewed.

The wider western masterplan must fill the measured connected dry land with
closely interlocking, individually composed masses and busy 2–3 m crooked lanes
to the real shore. House counts and a rectangular lot grid cannot prove coverage.
Existing stock anchors must be incorporated into new interiors or moved through
an independently reviewed real custody operation. No blanket rollback or silent
inventory recreation is part of this proposal.
