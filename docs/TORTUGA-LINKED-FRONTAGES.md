# Individually authored Tortuga frontages

The new recipe source models thirteen different compact buildings rather than
reusing the rejected extension's box/roof-striping builder. Taller hooked homes,
low glazed work wings, hips, crooked returns and smaller recessed entrances
form different masses. Their windows have real return glazing, proud occupied
bays, continuous head framing and carved supports. Ceilings have actual shallow
joists and small mounted task lights. Native fancy windows and modeling blocks
provide depth; material alternation alone is not the architectural treatment.

The trades are a lantern keeper, glass/chart shop, seamstress, net mender, ship
joiner, supper house, baker, small music tavern, mariner, bookbinder, tea/mending
house, tackle shop and the smaller Thimble & Salt. Each has genuine working
stations, detailed household scenes, ordinary usable stairs and retained door
halves. Floor furniture stays inside the shell, rather than replacing walls.
All containers are new native defaults; no existing Items, owners, professions,
crafting history or living entities are fabricated.

The first executable source cluster is Tideglass and Thimble & Salt on the
measured northern headland. Their different depths and widths flank a real
two-wide crooked lane. The smaller door recess and porch cheeks remain inside
its body; the glass shop's lane-side casement supports are raised above the
literal two-block walking volume. Four furnished scenes are available for
actual street/workshop/home inspection.

The source producer authenticates the exact stopped FULL save and protective
source masks. Every complete main/stoop/path footing is dry and traced through
the entire gravel stack to qualified non-falling natural substrate. Water or
AIR under a gravel crust refuses the site. Retained substrate verification
includes the complete stack; no water gap is filled. Full current before proof
also includes unchanged walking AIR. Fresh native before/support checks,
placement, normal joins, strict complete state/NBT verification and real
eye-level/interior images remain required.

This first pair and the other modeled recipes do not complete the western
village. They need individually composed fixed parcels, connected actual lanes,
further waterfront work and photo-led refinement across the residual land.
Geometry counts and local checks are not native survival, collision, artistic
acceptance or full-village coverage.
