# Architectural depth across the entire settlement

The owner requests a new structural depth layer on **every building and structure**,
covering exterior walls, roof edges and sensible interior walls and ceilings.
Flat material substitutions alone do not satisfy this request.

The design vocabulary includes recessed portals and niches, projecting framed
windows, supported bays and balconies, corbels, layered arches and cornices,
tapering buttresses, articulated eaves, trusses, ceiling coffers and vault ribs.
Each structure needs an intentional composition appropriate to its purpose.
Repeated identical trim, blanket thick awnings and noise pasted over every surface
do not establish the requested result.

| Working group | Required coverage |

| Signal Lane | All ten trade buildings, covered passage and connecting court |
| Rose & Lantern Court | Couple's cottage, bakehouse, weaver and glasshouse |
| Bramble Lane | All six workshops, loggia and their street-facing details |
| Rosewater and mill | Castle wings, audience room, mill and wheelhouse |
| River crossings | Main bascule banks, control towers and refined leaves; rose crossing |
| Maritime district | Original ornate tavern, net-maker, pilot shelter, repair court, earlier harbor buildings, quays and authored ship |
| Requested originals | Adapted Pirate Tavern and Fisherman's Shop; preserve their recognizable source silhouettes and attribution |
| Other authored structures | Review remaining settlement structures and connect their coverage to actual source and native evidence |

The owner's material rules still apply to complete buildings, including roofs and
floors: at most one Cartesian axis may have a same-material run above three, and
no uninterrupted run may exceed nine in any axis. Block-facing changes are not
material breaks. Authored outdoor surfaces retain the nine-block cap.

Keep existing inventories, workstation access, window and door operation, stairs,
street headroom, machinery, drawbridge sweep and open river navigation usable.
Installed mod models must be examined in the actual game; a nominally small model
can project outside its block. All construction precedes the final habitat and
population pass.

This is a coverage requirement and an active design pass, **not a completion
claim**. Each group still needs exact native placement, real game inspection and
corrections wherever the new geometry reads poorly. Geometry counts, material
checks and copied reference images cannot establish artistic acceptance.
