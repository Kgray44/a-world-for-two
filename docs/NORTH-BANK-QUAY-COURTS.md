# Remaining quay courts

The first bounded residual-land batch has six qualified original houses: Sea-Kite
House, Cask & Star, Albatross Ink, Bronze Wren, Tallow & Petal and Pilot’s Kin.
Their twelve rooms have real workstations, beds, pantry groups, contained plants,
reading objects, compact lamps and modeled ceiling ribs. Varied cropped/recessed
footprints, transverse gables, hips and gambrels give the small sea-edge streets
different silhouettes. Exact saved support/body checks precede each proposal.

Six other attempted fields remain refused because they meet actual water,
retained objects or incompatible saved ground. They are preserved in the private
manifest; the producer does not fill the shore, erase a tree/BE, weaken an old
AIR/solid expectation or repeat shifts until a box happens to fit.

`tools/artistic_north_bank_quay_courts.py` emits only entirely qualified houses,
exact before/support proofs and bounded normal-join targets. Native placement,
strict verification, public-lane connections and actual eye-level day/night
review remain separate. This batch is not a claim that every northern gap is
filled or that the new art is accepted.
