# Retained-stock West fifteen: Tortuga shell correction

This is a proposed exterior layer for the fifteen rejected western expansion buildings. It removes the bright periodic roof courses and adds actual recessed or projecting architecture. It preserves their current contents and usable interiors. Keeping a building's stock in place does not accept its old layout or establish that the approved city plan is complete.

`tools/artistic_tortuga_west15_shells.py` resolves the original fifteen construction sources by their pinned manifests, then reads their actual states and full typed block-entity data from a specified authenticated, stopped checkpoint. It emits three five-building packets, each below 12,000 placement targets, with exact before/full verification plans, a normal-neighbor-settle plan and a clearly labeled overhead planning map. No existing block entity, item, slot, owner or container tag is a placement target. Unknown loot-table contents remain unknown. Complete current typed NBT is kept only in the private packet.

The roofs use one subdued dark-oak or spruce family per real main/cross plane. Half-hip terminal faces form a continuous secondary plane, rather than periodic colored bands. The steep native roof keeps its profile with moss-slate pieces. Original copper stripe stairs keep their actual facing, half and shape while changing to dark timber; the high copper coping becomes shaped stone. Bright opaque plaster becomes subdued brown or gray. Furniture, windows, doors, existing room floors and stored items remain in place.

Each building receives independently fitted geometric depth: carved half-block high-gable recesses, supported carved casement heads, projecting gable trusses, or contained planting ledges. The two hipped buildings can receive a short, broader structural timber crest where their blank gable faces cannot safely be carved. The fittings are qualified against actual saved air, full backing, actual block entities and their supports, accepted heritage, every neighboring main footprint, and the newer complete construction deltas. A refused fitting is omitted; no occupied space is cleared to force it into place. The complete current building must pass the material rule after the change.

## Exact native roof joins

The source recipe is bound to `mcw-roofs-2.3.2-mc1.21.1neoforge.jar`, SHA-512 `c0e82a3d0a3ab2f2fac5fb0bdd7c7c228f084feaa816540d3d9524f341c8b108c3bb1afecadae2e8118e6c93f0e73280c62da1af4349aca87b6aa337e5e22ae4`. Read-only inspection of the pinned `BlockInit` suppliers identifies spruce, dark oak, brown terracotta, red terracotta, bricks and stone bricks as `BaseRoof` classes. The original interop recipe follows `BaseRoof.getStairsShape`, `canTakeShape` and `isBaseRoof`: a same-half front/back BaseRoof with perpendicular facing produces the corner, with the side veto comparing against that candidate roof. Material identity does not prevent these six classes from joining. Other roof classes are not guessed to share the rule.

Consequently, an old source state's derived `shape` may differ from its current native shape without implying an owner edit. Such a cell is eligible only when its block identity and every other property still match, the actual neighboring primary identities/facings/halves remain intact, and the shape independently derived from the complete physical mask exactly equals the saved state. Ridge `part` receives the equivalent narrow, already established derivation. Arbitrary state differences are retained. The new complete geometry determines the expected final joins; no observed failure is copied into accepted expectations.

## Admission and remaining evidence

Prepare with both `--checkpoint-id` and `--checkpoint-sha`. The reader checks the storage reserve, available memory, exact stopped receipt, bounded FULL chunk data, pinned roof artifact and finite time/output limits before issuing source packets. It performs no native actions.

The parent must freshly verify the exact before states and all typed custody, place only the non-BE proposal, apply normal neighbor updates, and strictly verify the complete resulting states and unchanged inventories. Actual eye-level, roof and interior screenshots are still required. Source geometry and focused tests establish neither native placement nor artistic acceptance. This layer needs no inventory relocation; any later change to the underlying layout requires a separate custody-reviewed operation.
