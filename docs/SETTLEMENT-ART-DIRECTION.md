# The Lakeside Homestead: art direction

The permanent settlement keeps its existing plaza, cottage, gardens, castle and
Lantern Tree. The owner's cinematic detail pass is preserved verbatim in
`ADDENDUM-CINEMATIC-MASTERPIECE.md`.

The additional waterfront direction is a substantial pirate seaside town inspired
by the atmosphere of Tortuga in *Pirates of the Caribbean*. This is an original
Minecraft environment, using the installed pack's materials and existing coast.

Lanternwater's oceanfront and river mouth become two connected working quays:
weathered timber and plaster, irregular rooflines, jettied upper floors, shutters,
covered balconies, a crowded chandlery, sail loft, shipwright, captain's chart room,
fish market, warehouses and a furnished sailor's tavern. Piles support buildings
over water; narrow boardwalks, stepped streets and lanterns connect them. A clear
navigation channel and usable berths take precedence over filling the river.

The inland transition stays gentle: a welcoming home and fairytale gardens lead
down to a lively port. The residential area remains peaceful. No hostile pirate
spawns, live explosives or loaded artillery belong among the homes and shops.
Small discoveries and a reachable treasure trail reward exploration.

Construction plans are reproducible design inputs. Native placement, orientation,
inventory, vehicle operation, persistence and shader screenshots have separate
acceptance records. Block counts alone do not establish artistic quality.
