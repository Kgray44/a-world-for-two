# One continuous eastern village

The remaining 22 eastern houses are modeled, furnished construction source:
eight around Tidefold, eight through Lantern Fold and six on East Hook. They join
Rose Reach and Compass Bend through three crooked public lanes, three narrow
cross alleys and individually graded doorsteps. These names describe the village
to explore; they do not designate separated phases with empty lawns between.

The 55 rooms provide households as well as net-mending, dressmaking, glasswork,
toy carving, herbs, candles, weaving, harbor accounts, pottery and stonework.
Native modded furniture, trade displays, small lamps, plants and ceiling ribs
fill the rooms while preserving two-high routes. Roofs combine low hips,
asymmetric saltboxes, gambrels, transverse gables and slim steep slate profiles.
Several upper rooms are physically set back behind guarded galleries. Fancy
windows, corbels, recessed entries, small supported hoods and articulated ceiling
models provide depth. North/east houses rotate their real rooms and asymmetrical
footprints, rather than placing a different front door on an unchanged interior.

terrain; river water, natural trees and unknown terrain are refused. Nine buried

neither mined nor replaced.
