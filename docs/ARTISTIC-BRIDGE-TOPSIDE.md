# Lanternwater bridge topside ornament

The intended composition keeps the slender working55-degree bascule and its
clear ten-block walking lane. The moving leaves gain three short kinds of raised
iron/timber crest, carved cheeks around their retained quarterspan lights, a
supported central lamp standard and flush oak compass lozenges. The four fixed
bank standards gain scroll-supported carved cheeks, modeled limestone crowns,
outward guardians and warm wall fittings. These are real relief and silhouettes;
they do not turn the whole side into an opaque high wall or a random texture grid.

The original source is `tools/artistic_bridge_topside_ornament.py`. It is a private
proposal producer and never runs Minecraft, changes blocks/entities, installs an
engineering plan, creates glue, accepts an admission or invokes a command.
Every output remains construction-forbidden. Block counts and source material
checks are not artistic acceptance; actual eye-level and night views are required.

## What is kept

The entire X1188..1197/Y71..74 body corridor, thin Y71 walking top, underside,
complete bearing root rows and final two nose rows remain unchanged. No drive,
radio, button, approach, old light, furnishing, window or saved/default block
entity is a delta target. Moving relief remains inside the existing leaf boxes,
Y69..73, with no new over-channel fixed support. Existing inventory or kinetic
NBT is never replayed from a historical plan.

Moving details can use only **exact state strings** already present on the old
actually cycled leaves. An actual stopped-save representative of every selected
state must exist without a BE before a proposal is emitted. This preserves the
pinned known state forms of Create, Create Deco, Dawn of Time, Mythrais and the
vanilla slab/ironwork/light palette. Fixed standards can use richer sculpted
Mythrais/Dawn models and Macaw wall fittings without making those models part of
a moving contraption. A block ID alone is not treated as a movability witness.

The producer reads the exact current stopped checkpoint, proves both real
bearings are closed/disassembled and uses actual saved preimages. Saved AIR is
read outside the older sparse bridge plan rather than assumed. It checks the
hard3/9 material rule over each **complete** leaf and the complete bridge, and
the outdoor nine-block cap. State-facing changes do not count as material breaks.

New fixed voxels must clear a conservative55-degree sweep of the existing glue
prisms. Half-degree samples inflate by0.15m; a <30m vertex radius bounds the
unsampled half-step displacement below0.131m. This is a conservative source voxel
enclosure, not a native collision, model-bound or vessel-clearance result.
