# Comfortable future interiors

For buildings placed after this instruction, design regular main rooms around
**five clear blocks of interior width** where sensible. Five is a comfort target
with design discretion, rather than a universal hard constraint. The hard
minimum for a regular main interior is **three clear blocks at every point**
after outer walls and room dividers.

Measure the actual structural room, not its exterior bounding box. A five-block
outside wall span commonly leaves only three blocks inside. Posts, dividers,
projecting architecture and real access geometry must be considered. Furnish
the resulting room deliberately; a nominal width alone does not prove usable
circulation or native model clearance.

Real stairs, door thresholds and specifically designed small service features
may have sensible narrow exceptions. Identify their exact purpose and extent.
A narrow enclosed bedroom or ordinary workroom cannot become compliant by
renaming the whole room a service nook. Where a future wing is too narrow,
widen it, physically merge it into a suitable adjacent room, or redesign a
genuinely small projection as an open porch, balcony or bay with appropriate
furnishings.
