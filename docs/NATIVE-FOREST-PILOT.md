# Native woodland pilot

The first forest batch is three real sapling plots: birch at `(1080,67,-1088)`, magnolia at `(1080,67,-1144)`, and larch at `(1032,66,-1128)`. These lower-bank openings qualify the native growth workflow before wider planting from the river to the inland cliff. They are part of the measured connected south bank; they do not represent the entire woodland.

The source producer reads the immutable stopped checkpoint and captures every block in each normal/rare crown, deep-root envelope, and eight-block horizontal read/physics perimeter. Magnolia retains its large secondary form; larch retains its large form and deep roots. Missing or non-FULL data, existing canopy, block entities, crops, paths, wet roots, and protected architectural columns refuse a plot. No tree is cut, ground flattened, or variant forced.

All authored AIR is protected alongside solid construction. The keepouts include the existing town and mechanical district, woodland walks, future R5 fountain, western v2 zipline and its stations/frames/cliff approach, future bridge ornament, placed Rose Reach, and the proposed western shops. Old straight/v1 cable reservations are superseded. Future masks remain explicitly unplaced evidence.

The private output contains three exact before volumes, three sapling plans, the sealed R3 qualifications, a combined protection mask, and a review recipe. It creates no executable native request or construction-readiness approval. The actual45 compiled tree, scope, mixin and bee guards, a fresh current checkpoint and exact plot-scoped review remain parent-owned admission gates. Other construction can continue outside those three plots; this pilot cannot certify whole-town completion.

`bind_requests` authenticates installed candidate45/bridge0.6.31 and hashes its actual tree, scope, mixin and bee class bytes without loading them. Its default output is a binding receipt with no request. Only three explicit parent-issued readiness files, each scoped `one-exact-saved-tree-plot` and bound to that plot's current plan/checkpoint, allow it to prepare three separate native grow requests. It never creates those approvals, installs a request, launches Minecraft or calls a world command. Native runtime checks remain independent of preparation.

Native Bee R2 is mandatory. Birch and magnolia each reserve capacity for one native nest and up to three naturally generated bee occupants, six bee slots in total. These are budget reservations, not spawned animals or promised nests. Larch reserves none. Actual native outcomes and any occupants must enter the later LIFE census before its cohort is selected. Failed growth retains its real refusal and exact recovery/quarantine evidence; native retries cannot be used to force a preferred variant.

No trees or LIFE entities have been placed by this producer. Normal native growth, restart, appearance, habitat suitability and broader forest acceptance require separate real-world evidence.
