# Physical depth for the palace and Copper Wren

This is a separate layer over the actually placed palace, audience-chamber R4
and Copper Wren R3. It prepares exact saved preimages and private deltas; it never
edits a world, starts a game, operates machinery or spawns entities. Existing
source plans, successful and failed receipts, and earlier private proposals remain
unchanged. No new artistic acceptance is inherited from an older build.

The design adds real geometry. Existing masonry can be adopted as a backing or
joint, but changing its texture does not count as depth. Every named building
mass must gain occupied positions outside its previous surface, and all new
geometry needs a face-connected path to retained solid construction. The latter
is a source support-path check, not a substitute for actual model/collision views.

The palace gets five shallow oriels in distinct places: the royal north gallery,
north guest room, eastern service wing, south music bay and existing west archive
bay. Actual saved Macaw glazing moves one block outward with its identity,
plane and other supplied properties retained. Neighbor-derived parts use the
exact pinned Macaw 2.4.2 rule: the increasing-coordinate first edge is R.
A saved old KNOWN_SHAPE placeholder is not treated as a canonical joined part.
Its old plane becomes a recess. Continuous column cheeks,
two-block sills, rooted corbels and stepped arched crowns produce shadows and
thickness. The service-wing arch follows its east-facing width in Z, rather than
rotating the facade while leaving its stairs pointed along the wrong axis.

Undercut string courses follow the actual royal structure, rotunda, guest and
service wings, ballroom and gate ensemble. Existing openings retain their own
dressed heads; no band covers a window or doorway. Engaged royal pilasters attach
to existing ashlar, with corbel roots and deliberate floor breaks. Both smaller
lodges gain two-block timber hoods and bracket profiles above their entries,
preserving their existing standing/headroom lanes. Existing archive buttresses,
deep gate portal, cloister arches, inhabited bays and R4 audience coffers remain.
The actual reviewed R4 ceiling already has recessed panels and shaped ribs;
this layer does not replace that successful interior with another flat roof.

At the mill, the four high attic glazing cells become paired real Macaw glass
doors opening onto a two-block projecting balcony. Source inspection established
that this high window had no floor behind it. The layer therefore includes a
full supported climbing ladder, an attached load post and a guarded interior
landing, not merely an inaccessible pair of doors. The ladder starts on the
actual hall floor and reaches the new landing below the clear roof volume.
Corner brackets support the exterior floor; actual railings and mounted lights
finish its edges. This remains a normal ladder/door/walking route, with no fake
movement or teleport claimed. Native climb and player collision remain a gate.

A higher timber window hood gives the lower mill facade depth while preserving
the two-wide castle-to-yard path beneath it. Low projecting cheeks were rejected
because they entered that actual route. Secondary physical ceiling joists cross
above the retained transverse trusses. Original mill route floors and their two
standing/body blocks remain fixed; the new joists sit above that declared
clearance. There is no alteration of cargo, grain-chest lids, machine access,
gears, sealed water volume, pneumatic route, existing tube links or train state.
All saved block entities and inventories are protected before each proposal cell.

Both complete composed building groups must retain the owner's material-ID rule:
no same-material run over nine and at most one Cartesian axis over three. Facing
changes do not break a material run. The wider authored mill quarter also retains
the outdoor nine-block cap. Existing native water and generated tube body cells
are explicitly recognized inside their approved machine envelopes and remain
untouched. Two existing natural vines in the old castle air volume remain fixed.
No unknown predecessor identity is silently accepted.

Every changed coordinate receives its exact authenticated saved state, including
the glazing that is moved or converted. The tool records each retained source
hash, native partial-machine context, new occupied positions, building coverage,
support checks and intended new access. Existing BEs, furnishings, doors and
declared circulation refuse edits. Stair corners are explicitly pending normal
native joining; intended facing and half remain specified. No LIFE seeding,
source commands or new entities are part of this architectural step.

The native sequence remains a fresh exact preimage check, bounded addressed
placement, real-level neighbor settling and complete readback, then actual game
views of each new oriel/hood and the mill balcony/ladder. Observe changed window
parts, actual decorative column joins, rail collision and door travel rather
than assuming the source geometry proves their behavior. The new geometry and
material checks are evidence of a coherent proposal, not a declaration that the
town is artistically finished.
