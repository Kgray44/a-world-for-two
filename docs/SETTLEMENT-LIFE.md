# The inhabited landscape

The owner's latest expansion requires the pirate town to continue across the
entire actual spit between the river and ocean, including its narrow seaward tip.
Its boundary must follow surveyed dry land. The navigation channel, bascule sweep,
bridge underspan and approaches remain clear. Dense streets give way to smaller
pilot, fishing and lookout places at the tip; the coast is not replaced by one
uniform platform or a repeated row of houses. Existing complete-building 3/9 and
outdoor nine-block material rules continue to apply.

After construction, functional commissioning and the visual passes are complete,
the **whole new area** receives an inhabited landscape pass. This is required
work, not a claim that entities have already been placed. Plants, trees, insects,
aquatic creatures, domestic animals, cats, dogs, villagers and suitable magical
residents need actual habitats and reasons to occupy each district.

The **entire south bank** must become a beautiful woodland filled with wildlife.
Determine its actual dry-land extent from the saved river geography. Create a
broad, connected forest with mixed canopy heights and species, understory,
ferns, flowers, glades, woodland paths and distinct wet river-edge habitats.
Weave it around the cottages, workshops and castle while keeping their views,
entrances, usable outdoor spaces and machinery clear. Avoid a uniform plantation
or a thin decorative tree border. Wildlife must inhabit the forest naturally,
with small appropriate groups, suitable shelter, water and food habitats.
This forest is part of the final LIFE pass after the architectural depth work.

| Place | Environmental and resident direction |
| --- | --- |
| Cottage courts and quiet gardens | Flowering borders, herbs, layered shrubs, small appropriate trees, butterflies or other installed insects, cats, household activity |
| Craft streets and market courts | Shopkeepers with reachable jobs and homes, sheltered seating, provisions, useful animals and changing little street scenes |
| Pirate quay, taverns and shipyard | Maritime workers and traders, cats, fishing activity, coastal plants and suitable shore creatures |
| River margins and ponds | Reeds, water plants, safe banks, small fish, frogs and appropriate peaceful wetland animals |
| Ocean shore and underwater gardens | Submerged vegetation, suitable fish groups and peaceful installed aquatic species at their actual depth and habitat |
| Castle, woodland and magical gardens | Horticultural variety, appropriate woodland wildlife and verified peaceful magical residents with usable spaces |
| Narrow seaward tip | Wind-shaped planting, a few inhabitants associated with lookout or fishing work, coastal wildlife and clear sea views |

The installed native registry, not a guessed mod ID, is authoritative. It already
contains candidate families such as Fish of Thieves and Hybrid Aquatic fish,
Critters and Companions, improved cats, Doggy Talents, vanilla villagers and Ribbits.
Registration alone does not prove safe behavior or a suitable habitat. Hostile
pirates and harmful ocean species belong outside the protected home environment.
Decorative fish mounts, projectiles, plushies and furniture must never be confused
with living residents merely because their entity IDs contain an animal name.

The implementation must place residents in small, spatially spread groups with
real walking space, beds/worksites or species-appropriate support and water.
Trees must have room to grow without obstructing mechanisms or the river; use
stable contained plants beside moving machinery. Pet ownership is preserved
through normal gameplay rather than assigning someone else's account silently.

An explicit uniquely tagged population manifest will prevent repeated runs from
duplicating residents. Existing unrelated inhabitants are preserved. Inspect the
result at eye level and below water, then compare active-area and empty-server
entity counts, loaded chunks, CPU/RAM and MSPT. Tune density and habitat placement
from those observations while retaining the owner's requested lively atmosphere.
No blanket permanent chunk loading or artificial no-AI population substitutes
for inhabitants behaving naturally.
