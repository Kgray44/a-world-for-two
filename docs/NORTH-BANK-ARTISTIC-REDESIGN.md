# North-bank redesign and the final depth pass

The owner rejected the new north-bank extensions after viewing them in Minecraft:
their bright roofs, repeated rows and gaps did not match the accepted original
pirate lane. Placement and durability records for those extensions remain valid
construction history; they are **not artistic approval**. The accepted original
section and the owner's subsequent play progress must be preserved.

The replacement must use closely interlocking, uneven buildings around crooked
human-scale lanes. Favor weathered dark timber, worn gray masonry, muted plaster,
restrained tiled roofs and varied silhouettes. Fill the connected northern dry
land to its water edges, preserving the river and bridge passage. Main building
footprints stay on dry ground; small supported overhangs and docks may project.
Existing inventories, functional fixtures, routes and placed zipline frames are
hard exclusions until an explicit, contents-preserving relocation is prepared.

The first replacement is a compact three-frontage study with doors facing
different directions, a bent lane, seven furnished rooms and layered façades.
Its source checks do not qualify its appearance. Place and inspect this first
group through actual Minecraft eye-level, roofline and interior screenshots,
refine it, then extend the successful architectural language. Do not repeat the
rejected bright-row plan or use decorative plants to hide that layout.

## Current material rule

The owner's latest instruction permits **unlimited identical blocks along one
axis when the design benefits**, especially fences, walkway borders, beams and
roof edges. Artificial color stripes or arbitrary nine-block seams are not
required. Within each complete building, the same block material still may not
exceed three consecutive blocks in more than one Cartesian axis. Facing/state
changes do not count as different materials.

Outdoor checks allow long continuous borders and retain breakup of broad plain
fields. The former unconditional nine-block cap in earlier proposal records is
historical and superseded. Geometry checks never certify artistic quality.

## Full 3D pass and lived-in detail

An additional full depth pass remains required across **every building and
structure**, including the accepted sections. Shape appropriate flat walls,
ceilings, eaves and other surfaces with real recessed/projecting construction:
casements and sills, brackets, porch frames, bays, balconies, dormers, rafters,
coffers and fitted furniture. Keep supported connections, useful headroom,
doorways, alleys, machinery and navigation clear. Do not add unsupported clutter
or obstruct functional blocks just to increase detail counts.

Decorations and atmosphere require at least the attention given to shells:
trade-specific displays, sheltered seating, signs, working lamps, ropes,
household objects and layered planting. The imported fisherman’s shop needs a
complete fitted interior around both existing entrances, not a substitute shop.
Botanical LIFE and the separately admitted animal/resident LIFE pass remain
required after construction, including the dense south-bank forest to the cliff.

The owner's Captain’s Head reference calls for a few distinctive halls with
heavy timber framing, muted plaster, shaped tiled annexes, deep porches, broad
casements, nautical signs and lush planting. Primary artist references include
[Captain’s Head by Sophie Ingledew](https://sophieingledew.artstation.com/projects/ely5bb),
[Port Merrick by Jamie Read](https://jamieread3d.artstation.com/projects/8bezBG),
and [Port Merrick interiors by Joachim Coppens](https://www.joachimcoppens.com/work/sea-of-thieves-port-merrick).
These are design references, never evidence of the Minecraft result. Film-set
details can also be studied in [Lauren Polizzi’s Pirates set-design work](https://laurenpolizzi.com/set-design/pirates-of-the-carribean-k6msp).

Bridge, fountain, zipline, ships, train/rail loop and tube demonstrations still
need real operation and persistence as well as visual quality. No static replica,
source bundle, block count or synthetic test substitutes for those requirements.
