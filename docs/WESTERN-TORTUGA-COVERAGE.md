# Western Tortuga coverage

The western expansion remains incomplete. The fifteen retained western houses,
three newer W3 frontages and Captain's Rest occupy 1,633 distinct main/doorstep
columns. Adding the frozen florist, two trade frontages and three open working
spaces gives 2,036 columns. Those metadata totals are about 14% and 18% of the
historical 11,571-column north-connected western bank; they are diagnostic
figures, not art scores, fresh dry-plot authority or a target density.

`tools.artistic_west_coverage` independently compares exact main footprints
against that one-metre bank locator, then checks current stopped FULL ground,
literal supports, body volume, block entities and existing/future source masks.
It reports connected residual pockets and fully clear 7×7 diagnostic fields.
These rectangles are never automatic lots or houses. Missing terrain, water,
current stores, accepted town, paths, future flight, roofs and working spaces
cannot be treated as empty land. Reservation-only AIR is a protective mask,
not physical building geometry or a counted floor.

The next authored frontages must make one continuous village: narrow crooked
lanes, tightly interlocking muted timber/stone/plaster masses, small useful
courts and meaningful work at the shoreline. Twelve distinct irregular
architectural recipes may support individually composed parcels; a single
repeated box, roof stripes or an empty area relabelled greenery does not meet
the brief. Each actual building still needs fully modeled interiors, genuine
stairs and doors, three-dimensional windows/ceilings, fresh preimages and real
native street/interior views before broader replication.
