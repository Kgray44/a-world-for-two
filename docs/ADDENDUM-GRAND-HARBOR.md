# A WORLD FOR TWO
## Grand Harbor, Historic Sailing Ships & Tortuga-Style Pirate Port Addendum

**Repository:** https://github.com/Kgray44/a-world-for-two

**Priority:** HIGH — Apply this to the ongoing permanent-world settlement build before final acceptance.

This addendum strengthens and expands the harbor, pirate-town, and maritime-world requirements.

## CORE DIRECTIVE

I want the ocean-side portion of the settlement to become a **major, true port environment** — bustling, dramatic, beautiful, and historically inspired.

This is **not** a small decorative marina.

This should feel like a real **17th–18th century wooden sailing harbor**, combined with a lively pirate port in the spirit of **Tortuga**: busy docks, waterfront buildings, working ship infrastructure, small craft, warehouses, taverns, rope and cargo areas, visible maritime life, and large true-to-style wooden ships.

The port must be one of the most impressive and memorable parts of the entire settlement.

---

## 1. LARGE HISTORIC WOODEN SHIPS — MANDATORY

Build multiple **large, true-to-life, historically inspired wooden sailing ships** based on the overall build, layout, silhouette, rigging style, and deck arrangement of real 17th–18th century vessels.

These do **not** need to be exact museum reproductions, but they must look plausibly authentic to the era and should avoid cartoonish or generic fantasy-ship proportions.

### Historical style goals

Ships should reflect believable age-of-sail design characteristics such as:

- Long wooden hulls with proper curvature
- Raised stern sections where appropriate
- Realistic bows and bowspirit areas
- Masts positioned believably
- Full sailing-rig silhouette
- Realistic deck layouts
- Visible rigging style suggestions
- Stern windows / quarterdeck / forecastle style where appropriate
- Believable anchor placement
- Cargo and deck equipment
- Ship boats / skiffs / hoists
- Aged, detailed, premium craftsmanship

These vessels should look like ships that genuinely belonged in the **golden age of sail / piracy / colonial maritime era**.

---

## 2. REQUIRED SHIP LAYOUT

### A. Offshore moored large ship

Build **one large primary ship a bit offshore**, moored in a believable way.

Requirements:

- Positioned away from the dock, anchored or moored offshore
- Sails **partially unfurled** as if drying
- Visually dramatic and highly detailed
- Several small usable skiffs at the dock or in the water nearby
- The skiffs should serve as tender boats used to reach the main ship
- The main ship should include a **hoisting system** to lift a skiff aboard, inspired by real ship’s davits / tackle / hoist behavior
- The hoist area should look believable and detailed
- The offshore ship should feel like an active working vessel rather than a static prop

### B. Three large docked ships

Build **three additional large ships docked along the ocean-facing harbor edge**.

Configuration:

1. **Ship One:** sails fully furled
2. **Ship Two:** sails fully furled
3. **Ship Three:** sails fully let out / loosened and hanging visibly, being lightly blown by the wind as if drying, not tightly set for travel

Each of these ships should feel distinct in silhouette and detailing.

Do not simply duplicate the same vessel four times.

Vary:

- Hull form
- Stern design
- Deck arrangement
- Color accents
- Figurehead style if used
- Sail arrangement
- Cargo or deck clutter
- Purpose and atmosphere

### C. Smaller ships and boats

Also build several smaller period-appropriate vessels, including:

- A few smaller wooden ships
- 1 or 2 smaller craft near the harbor mouth
- 1 smaller vessel somewhere on the river
- Several rowboats / skiffs / service craft
- Small fishing or transport craft where appropriate

These smaller vessels should help the harbor feel active and alive.

## 3. THE PORT MUST FEEL LIKE A REAL PORT

The pirate/ocean side of the settlement should become a **major maritime district** — not just ships placed near land.

Build a substantial waterfront town inspired by **Tortuga**:

- Docks and piers
- Harbormaster area
- Rope and sail loft areas
- Warehouses
- Cargo yards
- Crane / hoist infrastructure
- Fish market
- Sailmaker / chandlery style areas
- Tavern or inn
- Waterfront houses
- Captains’ quarters / offices
- Supply sheds
- Barrels, crates, rope piles, nets, cargo stacks
- Walkways, ladders, gangplanks
- Mooring posts and harbor detailing
- Lanterns and atmospheric lighting
- Coastal defenses or watch positions where appropriate

The district should feel **bustling, layered, detailed, and full of visual storytelling**.

I want this area to feel like a real pirate port settlement with substantial effort and architectural richness.

---

## 4. QUALITY EXPECTATION: TORTUGA ENERGY

The pirate-port section should feel:

- Busy
- Cinematic
- Dense with detail
- Rich with atmosphere
- Lively and adventurous
- Rougher and more weathered than the cottagecore inland areas
- Distinct in identity from the main homestead

This should be one of the most visually exciting sections of the world.

Think:

- Warm lantern light at night
- Rigging silhouettes against the sunset
- Crates and cargo on the dock
- Ships at different stages of readiness
- Water access points
- Boardwalks and alleys
- A little grime and age
- A “real port” feeling

Do not leave large empty dock zones or giant featureless water gaps.
