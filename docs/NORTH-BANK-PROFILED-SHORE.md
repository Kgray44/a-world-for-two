# Profiled sea-edge homes

Fourteen additional original houses qualified in the next bounded source batch:
eight along the western courts/sea edge and six on the eastern shore. Their 28
rooms include galley groups, real workstations, beds, reading corners, compact
wall lamps and modeled ceiling ribs. Cropped and recessed plans, native fancy
windows, shallow hips, gambrels, asymmetric roofs and transverse gables give the
small houses different street silhouettes.

Each candidate starts from an exact saved 9×11 dry/relief profile suitable for a
7×9 main footprint and one-metre model skirt. That surface evidence is followed
by a complete actual body, tree/BE, ownership, water and foundation check. It is
never substituted for a whole-body proof. Four other reference attempts remain
refused for actual foundation water, prior authored geometry, an obstructed
interior route or the eight-metre separation from native lava.

`tools/artistic_north_bank_shore_profiles.py` preserves the old/future houses,
corrected R2 alleys and exact cable/station envelope. It emits only the entire
qualified houses, exact before/support proofs and bounded normal-join targets.
Native placement, strict verification, public connections and eye-level review
remain separate. Source counts do not establish acceptance or complete coverage
of the northern village; the residual survey remains explicit about other gaps.
