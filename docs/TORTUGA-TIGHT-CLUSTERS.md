# Tight Tortuga building clusters

The next western source cluster puts four different inhabited masses together:
a low supper wing, tall bookbinder, offset tea/mending house and a seamstress
turning into the southern return. Their full interiors and stairs are retained.
The tea shop has a second west entrance opposite the bookbinder. The latter's
real fancy casement is taller, with its carved head and knees physically raised
above the two-high alley rather than ignored by collision checks.

The final fixed source geometry has 326 building/stoop columns and 70 narrow
lane columns. Earlier unplaced 264- and 173-lane studies remain preserved.
They are superseded; the village is not completed by paving remaining land.
These figures apply only to this bounded four-building cluster. The wider
western shore still needs further contiguous building masses and actual views.

Ground-level routes have a different authority from building plots. A complete
new building needs its full saved dry body/roof volume. A tiny public lane can
pass under a retained old eave only when its exact current floor, entire gravel
substrate, literal two-high body and existing source AIR are verified. Every
existing/future main footprint, store, working machine and accepted original
section stays protected. Retained pavers and walking AIR are witnesses rather
than rewrite targets. Source layout never authorizes native deployment.

One additional substrate is narrowly qualified from the exact installed Create
6.0.10 artifact: its pinned `AllBlocks` initializer registers `zinc_ore` using
the native `Block` constructor, not `FallingBlock`. The class hash, bootstrap
and constructor witness are bound to each proposal. Other mod materials, water,
AIR and unknown states remain refused. The original vanilla-only stability
helper and all failed gravel receipts stay unchanged.

Fresh native before/support checks, normal placement/joins, full state/NBT
verification, real walking and eye-level/interior images remain required.
The original small pirate section and all current inventories are preserved.
