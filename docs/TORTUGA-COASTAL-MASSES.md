The interiors include real working stations, backed wall storage, native-default cupboards, correctly facing seating, beds, normal stairs and small supported task lights. Different proportions and lower annexes provide variety without bright roof bands. The whole-building material rule passes, including the intentional single-axis exception. Those checks do not prove beauty or native model clearance.

Fitting uses a fresh authenticated stopped snapshot. Main floors require literal dry ground and the complete gravity chain to qualified native substrate. The earlier five's exact construction deltas remain excluded. The approved map's mass and lane area is a further planning limit; it does not add any terrain authority. Door directions and short lanes are fitted to real dry land: the galley faces inland, and the tiny shop's casement knees sit above the two-high common approach.

The small quay entrance borrows only an existing four-column cobbled public threshold. Its floor identity is checked against the actual prior ground delta, then reread together with both AIR body levels and absence of block entities from the current snapshot. This grants walking evidence only. It creates no new buildable cells, clears no existing fixture and replays no stored NBT.

Each private bundle contains separate placement, exact before, retained-support, complete-after and ordinary-neighbor-join files. Each also has `batch-map-PLAN-ONLY.svg`, drawn from the actual new main footprints and short lane cells. It is explicitly a source schematic. Parent-owned native before/after maps, placement, joins, complete verification, real street/interior captures and walking remain required.

The source process temporarily registers only its new recipe names and restores the original recipe dictionary even on failure. It does not register anything in Minecraft. Each native batch is capped at 12,000 new targets; the prepared three are far below that limit. Existing player progress, accepted architecture, mechanics, flight and inventories remain outside construction authority.
