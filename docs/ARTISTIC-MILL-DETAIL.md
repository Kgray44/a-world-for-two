# Copper Wren mill: separate R3 finish proposal

This is an architectural overlay for the actual placed R2 mill, not a machinery
replacement or a claim of graphical acceptance. The native R2 arrival, ledger
and wheelhouse screenshots exposed a floating ridge, a broad unbroken roof,
ordinary house glazing, a dark working hall and a visually plain hydraulic box.

The proposal adds two unequal projecting cross-gables, a continuous supported
ridge, a small capped chimney, a sheltered double-width threshold and planted
window sills. Actual Macaw framed windows and shutters replace house glazing.
A low green roof and timber observation frontage dress the waterwheel enclosure
from outside its existing seal. The two original inspection apertures surrounding
the pneumatic feed remain full glass, as does the entire sealed hydraulic wall.

Inside, strapped trusses support chandeliers and task lamps. Low tea and repair
counters, cups and pots, a book-lined bench alcove, a small rug and an under-stair
cupboard give the room useful inhabited edges. The central machine and entrance
aisles retain their original two-block width. This proposal adds no machines,
commands, live entities, automatic processing or river-side platform.

`tools/artistic_mill_detail.py` pins the exact immutable native-placed R2 plan and
manifest. It protects all source Create, Overpressure, Hypertube and Railways
blocks with a one-block clearance margin, saved inventories and block entities,
the entire hydraulic containment volume, source water, grain-chest lid space,
all declared walking-route body/support cells and the 252 native tube collision
cells. Every new target gets an exact saved-checkpoint predecessor. Unrelated
construction, trees, fluids and saved block entities cause refusal. Small outer
post footings may descend at most three blocks to actual dry natural ground.

The complete composed mill passes the material-ID rule: no run longer than nine,
and at most one Cartesian axis longer than three for each material. The entire
authored R2 quarter plus overlay also passes the outdoor nine-block cap. These
are source geometry checks; they do not establish native window models, light
levels, route feel or visual quality.
