# Carved fountain refinement47

The actual candidate47 flow and upper-quarter photographs show a working
fountain whose upper white drum still reads as broad flat masonry. The new
proposal adds attached relief and a stepped cornice to those exposed wall bays.
It does not replace or recolor the existing sculpture.

Six small stacks occupy the west, east and north faces. Each has a carved
travertine wall bracket, a scroll volute, a curved cornice and a short lower lip.
Two arrival-side lips use subdued patinated copper; the other four retain pale
carved stone. Two fern pots sit on existing crown corner slabs, clear of Mars.
The south maintenance face and its gate remain open. This is26 additions, not a
new platform or another tower.

The actual pinned Mythrais models have14 carved elements for each lower bracket,
12 for the scroll and14 for the cornice. The brackets project9/16block and meet
their retained wall backing; the curved cornice projects11/16block. Their actual
model ancestry, rotations, bounds and archive hashes are recorded privately.
The cornice's facing convention differs from the bracket's, so orientation is
chosen from the physical backing plane. Two full cube columns would not provide
the same profile. These model summaries are not native collision or appearance
acceptance.

The source preserves every original solid, all statue parts, pipes, pump,
kinetics, water channels, pool, wheel, controls and public walking bodies. Actual
decorative model bounds were checked against those functional keepouts. Six
synthetic checks cover the protected volumes, retained mounts, refused
replacement, touching versus intersecting model bounds and the pinned cornice
orientation. The complete retained fountain plus additions passes the material
and outdoor rules. Those checks do not establish artistic quality.

Candidate47's actual hydraulic test passed before this proposal: the real pump
filled the header, all four jets flowed, physical STOP drained the jets and
reopening resumed them. No header source or kinetic NBT was injected. This is a
pump-filled header feeding gravity cascades; a full header normally backpressures
the pump. It is not described as a perpetual closed hydraulic loop.

The operating fountain is now **wet**. The575 archive provides only historical
static AIR targets and retained wall/slab mounts. The proposal is construction
forbidden and includes no old full-fountain dry replay. Before admission, the
parent must obtain fresh exact native target/mount checks and bind the current
wet-world checkpoint, then place only the reviewed additions, settle their
normal shapes and verify them. Working water/control/statue preservation and
matching arrival, upper-quarter, east, north and night views remain required.
No world, entity, block entity, inventory, controller or runtime change was made
by this source task.
