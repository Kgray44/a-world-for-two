# Western quay shops

The western lane is an original compact cluster, with a crooked street and
individual back yards rather than a row of identical houses. It is part of the
requested north-bank expansion **after** current fountain/forest/zipline and
first LIFE work. These source proposals perform no native actions. Expansion
LIFE comes after the new construction and its visual review.

The five compositions in `tools/artistic_north_bank_west.py` are:

- **Marigold & Tide:** a pale pink/cream L-shaped florist and potting wing, a
  double-height shop, an accessible projecting cherry oriel, a wrapping gallery
  with real balustrades and stairs, displayed blooms/pots and a botanical bench.
- **The Rivet & Ember:** a low soot-colored brick forge with an off-centre hearth
  and chimney, a recessed double entrance, an open side sharpening canopy,
  normal anvil/smithing/grinding stations and a public repair counter.
- **The Black Gull Armoury:** a narrow chamfered red/gray shop with tall pointed
  modded glazing, a repair counter, reference shelves and a separate upstairs
  fitting/ledger workshop reached by a real guarded stair.
- **The Wren’s Keel:** a long carpenter/sailmaker hall, a projecting clerks’
  alcove and an open rear rib-bench yard on modeled timber frames. The small
  workpiece is a workpiece, not an assembled or physics-qualified ship.
- **The Lantern Wake:** a new small inn with a chamfered plan, a bracketed porch,
  pointed-window front parlour, a real galley, conversation islands and a rear
  reading gallery. It does not replace the beloved Drowned Rose interior.

Macaw windows are actual modded glazing; their sills, projecting brows and carved
supports cast physical shadows. Furniture, ledgers, cups, pots, warm fittings,
working benches, loom and stair landings are part of each complete composition.
Storage is explicitly empty until supplied. No merchant, pet or decorative entity
is invented or spawned. There is no color-noise pass or automatic template grid.

## Saved-site boundary

South owns the overall masterplan and east/north region; the agreed division is
west X≤1216 / east X≥1217. The west source also protects the exact prospective
zipline v2 XYZ mask, provisional rigging plots, new landing court, all prior
accepted town construction/AIR/BEs and their model-edge skirt. A growth skirt is
not blindly treated as a street ban; the actual construction mask remains
distinct from future whole-crown reservations.

Every main-building column must be actual dry FULL saved terrain. The producer
then reads every solid/AIR/roof/sill/body target. Missing chunks, water, trees,
unreviewed objects and saved BEs refuse the plot. Low foundations fill only AIR
or ordinary soft cover **above** retained ground; land is never flattened,
excavated or filled into water. Every public entrance has a normal half-step up
from the natural shore to the raised wooden floor.

The first tavern candidate was refused at a non-waterlogged dead coral on dry
gravel. That refusal remains immutable. The composition moved two metres west,
keeping the coral untouched and tightening the alley alongside the forge.

The current saved proposals have main floors64 on retained gravel63. Bounds and
thresholds in their private manifests are exact block coordinates, not a claim
about a player's standing height. South must qualify and connect the external
2–3m lanes and small courts from the actual one-metre dry field. Main buildings
never become waterfront fill; any future short dock/bay requires its own review.

## Evidence boundary

The hard3/9 rule runs across the complete building, including roofs, interiors,
projections and actual support foundations. A conservative source floor graph
connects entry thresholds, rooms, lofts and usable standing sides of work
stations. Exact declared door states may open normally; an unrelated block at
the same coordinate cannot inherit that exception. Wall lights need real backing.

These are source geometry/support checks, not native avatar collision,
interactions, client rendering, resident behavior or artistic acceptance. Every
output remains construction-forbidden pending masterplan agreement, earlier
work, actual native preimages/placement/normal settlement, complete state and
inventory proofs, and eye-level/day/night views of every alley, frontage and
furnished room. A block total is not a quality measure.

The existing Drowned Rose exterior is a separate follow-on correction. Its whole
interior, inventories, windows, routes and roof structure must remain protected;
no exterior proposal grants interior refurnishing authority.
