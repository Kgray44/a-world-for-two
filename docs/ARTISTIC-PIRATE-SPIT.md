# The Drowned Rose and Pilots' Walk

This is an original **staging proposal**, following the actual dry peninsula
north and northeast of Signal Lane. It has not been placed, walked or visually
accepted in Minecraft. Geometry and material checks cannot establish its beauty.
The earlier rejected settlement is not used as a building template.

The route starts at the existing street's east exit, descends three short steps,
bends between the new tavern's galley and a net-maker, and continues between the
two reserved original builds toward the real narrow tip. An eastern work path
goes around the shop reservation and the native willow's roots. It never fills
the river or creates a broad artificial platform across the shore.

The tavern interior is the primary design effort. A reversed stationary capstan
chandelier hangs from the actual roof, with an iron/wood coupling breaking its
long chain. Transverse curved timber ribs frame the nave. A barrel-backed bar,
contained galley, conversation tables, warm lanterns, green sconces and a real
piano stage occupy distinct working and social areas. An asymmetric gallery has
a five-rise stair, book-lined reading nook and navigator's table overlooking the
hall. The side bay, cropped corners, tall windows, roof joints and timber roles
supply deliberate variation rather than random material changes.

The design follows the observed original [Tavern of the Damned overview](https://joachimcoppens.artstation.com/projects/5XDGyJ)
by Joachim Coppens for its capstan, ribs and light pools; [The Captain's Head](https://sophieingledew.artstation.com/projects/ely5bb)
by Sophie Ingledew for its gallery, sea-fort character and social corners; and
[Sam Westall's tavern concept sheets](https://samwestall2.artstation.com/projects/4bPm5n)
for carved arches and barrel-backed counters. These pages and actual images were
reviewed. No artwork, game asset or downloaded schematic is copied into this
original source.

## Evidence and admission

`tools/artistic_pirate_spit.py` reads only the owner's explicitly identified
stopped staging archive, through the canonical backup path/hash/member verifier.
Saved region entries and individual chunks are authenticated and bounded. It
does not open live MCA files, read a LOD as terrain, interpolate a building's
foundation, invoke a server API or launch a game.

Every housing foundation column must have actual natural dry ground within two
blocks of the proposed floor. Ordinary native trees are retained around this
original composition; the tip roof has a flush western eave to avoid its canopy.
Wild plants are cleared only at exact authored targets. Unknown saved objects,
water and preexisting block entities refuse admission. Complete prior states
are included separately from the proposed states; unspecified positions are
never assumed to be air.

Every complete authored building passes the owner's material rule, ignoring
facing/state differences: no uninterrupted material run above nine, and at most
one axis per material above three. The full combined proposal, including paving,
drains, steps and work surfaces, also passes the outdoor nine-block cap.
Framing, short boards, copper seams, masonry joints and openings provide the
breaks. The checker never randomly recolors a failed structure.

Final route checks run after furniture and planting. They verify floor support,
two-high planned body clearance and a connected terrain-following outdoor graph
with steps of at most one block. The roots are routed around. These are source
checks, not proof of native collision, walking, climbing, cooking or music.

Preparation requires a fresh output directory and a finite ReserveGuard check
of 16 MiB on C: and 32 MiB on D:. The complete bundle is capped at 32 MiB. This
monitoring does not control unrelated external applications or existing writers.
The private plans contain world bindings and must never be committed.

```powershell
python -B -m tools.artistic_pirate_spit --home '<private artistic home>' --checkpoint-id '<reviewed stopped checkpoint>' --checkpoint-sha '<exact reviewed archive SHA256>'
```

The output contains separate tavern, net-maker, repair-court, pilot-shelter and
lane plans, each paired with its exact saved preimage. The manifest supplies
foundation evidence, imported-build reservations, source/registry hashes,
planned rooms/routes and actual-game capture poses. Any file named
`VERIFY-ONLY` is an unchanged natural support witness and must not be placed.

The parent must verify each native preimage, place the paired proposal, inspect
settled native state, walk the route and capture real street/interior/night
images. Particularly review the tavern's entrance, bar, chandelier and gallery;
roof and arch joints; furnished scale; lighting; both original-build interfaces;
the willow work court; and the continuous walk to the tip. Correct the actual
images before artistic acceptance. No NPCs, pets, wildlife or other life are
spawned here: that pass follows completion of all construction.
