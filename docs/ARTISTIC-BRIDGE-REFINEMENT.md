# Lanternwater: a lighter working bridge

This is a separate, recoverable architectural upgrade. Its 2,775 fixed targets
were placed in the staging world; the 1,130 moving changes remain unplaced.
The first fixed verification retained 32 Macaw horizontal-window edge-part
differences. A separate verification-only correction derives their native
right/left joining rule without replaying the placed architecture. Native
verification of that corrected expectation and owner visual acceptance remain
separate gates. The reference is the actual
candidate37 shader photograph `river-clearance.png`, SHA256
`10e329aa31efdb1fe4037b757703d5820c2953a4e9a55e6165d6aff52c9b499d`.
That image shows the repeated timber T caps and continuous dark underside that
this proposal removes.

The two leaves retain their pivots, twelve-block widths and Y71 walking surface.
Only the full-block bearing attachment rows remain full thickness. The rest of
the deck becomes top-slab timber bays, supported by thin Dawn of Time beams,
segmented Create girders and visible iron collars. Low Mythrais ironwork and a
few slender lantern posts replace the tall solid rail assemblies. Above-deck
solid counts fall from 136 to 66 on the north leaf and 128 to 64 on the south leaf;
those counts describe authored blocks, not measured opacity or visual quality.

Fixed architecture changes the approaches rather than masking the old profile:

The pinned rosebridge and craft-lane plans, including their reserved air, are
exclusions. Preparation authenticates a current stopped backup, checks the entire
existing physical master, confirms saved closed bearings, roots new bank piers
on actual saved solid ground and refuses saved BEs or unrelated construction in
any changed cell. Recess carving is restricted to existing authored masonry;
ordinary soil or seagrass replacements needed by the small new footing columns
are listed in the private preimage. Fossils, trees and unknown blocks are refused.
Complete leaf, pavilion and whole-bridge checks enforce the three/nine material
rule without orientation tricks or random recoloring.

`tools.artistic_bridge_refinement` writes distinct fixed and moving deltas,
exact saved preimages, a rollback artifact and a complete verification-only
plan. Neither delta loads BE tags. The full plan must never be placed. The new
physical hash invalidates the old glue, cycle, restart and vessel-clearance
authority: after reviewed placement, admit new glue bounds, inspect actual
captured blocks, complete two real button cycles, verify closure, restart
without setup and repeat the cycles. Preserve failed receipts and prove ticket
cleanup. No source or geometry check substitutes for those native operations.
