# The south-bank woodland

The LIFE pass follows construction, commissioning and actual visual review. Its
landscape should form a broad woodland around the cottage, craft and castle
districts: mixed-height canopy, flowering glades, shaded fern communities and
quiet river margins. A thin border or regular plantation will not meet this aim.

`tools.artistic_south_forest` prepares a finite original proposal from an
authenticated stopped-world backup. It reads exactly two saved terrain regions,
with bounded decoding and a 512 MiB memory allocation. Actual Squaremap tiles
provide composition context; saved **FULL** chunks establish terrain authority.
Neither black map areas nor Distant Horizons LODs authorize filling unknown land.
Four-block samples establish geography; every proposed tree then receives a
per-cell saved-ground, crown, root-collar and object-clearance check.

The present study separates the low inhabited terrace from the much higher
opposite riverbank. It follows the actual dry component containing the cottages.
It does not bridge water or extend into the western unsurveyed region. Unknown
or incomplete saved chunks, existing trees and retained objects stay untouched.
All current authored cells, **including AIR routes**, plus five metres are
excluded. The complete crown also stays five metres from saved objects, rather
than protecting only its centre. Every cell in that perimeter must be known;
an unknown saved cell fails admission even when the crown itself is dry.
Castle and mill depth/oriel additions are part
of that union. The complete river channel and bridge navigation/motion envelopes
remain clear.

The original palette uses installed Regions Unexplored magnolia, maple, silver
birch, larch, pine and, where a later wet-bank admission supports it, willow.
Trunks and branches connect; crowns vary in height, radius and shape. Persistent
native leaves avoid uncontrolled sapling growth beside buildings or machinery.
New trees require real dry soil, modest root slopes and actual empty crown
space. Existing canopy is preserved. There is no terrain flattening, water fill,
tree cutting or giant imported tree stamp. This is natural planting, not a way
to evade the architectural 3/9 material rules; no building geometry is changed.

The private proposal includes exact source provenance, tree profiles, empty
meadow pockets, understory patches and saved per-cell preimages. It is explicitly
**not an executable native plan**. A fresh postconstruction checkpoint, complete
exclusion custody, native leaf-neighbour and plant-survival expectations, parent
placement/verification and actual eye-level shader review remain necessary.
Woodland paths must follow actual dry contours and connect to the retained town
walks; no straight road or broad paving is assumed from the coarse sample grid.
Named low-canopy meadow corridors keep the cottage-to-castle approach and south
palace outlook open. Existing wet banks receive small orchid/fern communities
on their actual dry shore, with exact adjacent source-water witnesses. No new
pond, submerged obstruction or river fill is assumed. The new starting plaza
and fountain have a provisional no-plant reservation; their exact forthcoming
AIR, route, basin, plumbing and sightline mask must replace it before final binding.

## Wildlife after the landscape

The pinned Naturalist 2.0.7 JAR was inspected directly, not inferred from entity
names. Deer register panic, avoidance and grass-eating goals. Birds register
seed-eating, flocking and avoidance. Butterflies pollinate and can affect crop
growth. Fireflies hide in grass and wander in flight. Ducks swim and panic;
their native food tag accepts raw fish. Dragonflies use their native flight and
hover AI. These observations support small habitat proposals; they do not prove
native multiplayer behavior or imply that every `creature` category is harmless.
Predators, hostile creatures, spell entities, decorative mounts and unverified
magical residents are excluded from this initial woodland population.

Every proposed dry-glade group records its actual registry category and pinned
class-inspection hash, world-coordinate range and a saved local support/headroom
witness. Each glade has 25 inspected natural support columns and 75 body cells;
this is geometry evidence, not a native collision, survival or behavior pass.
The complete private species table retains those distinctions. Ducks and
dragonflies were inspected but remain candidates until their wet habitats have
equivalent depth, shore-exit and flight-space evidence.

Each proposed group has a retained population namespace, stable member UUIDs and
unique tags. Reordering proposals does not change identities. The later native
seed must preserve a world population ledger, finalize ordinary spawn variants
and AI, and verify actual support, body clearance, food and suitable habitat.
Absence from loaded chunks is not permission to duplicate a wandering resident.
No pet owner, tame state or `NoAI` population is assigned. Water-edge species and
fish need their own actual depth, shore-exit and behavior checks first.

No resident has been seeded by this tool. Native species interactions, habitat
use, eye-level and underwater appearance, entity counts, loaded chunks, CPU,
RAM and MSPT remain separate observations. No forced chunk loading or performance
claim accompanies the proposal.
