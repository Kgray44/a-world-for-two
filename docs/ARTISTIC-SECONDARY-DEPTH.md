# Secondary palace ceilings and bridge lodge returns

This separate original proposal addresses five specific omissions documented in
`ARCHITECTURAL-DEPTH-COVERAGE-AUDIT.md`. It does not replace the palace, moving
bridge, existing fixed towers or their successful silhouettes. It does not claim
the town visually accepted.

The frozen proposal has **93 targets**:85 additions in actual saved air and eight
explicitly reviewed white-terracotta wall cells. Each blind alcove uses a bottom
and top stair facing inward, preserving the wall's solid inner half while making
a real half-block external recess. No material-only recolor counts as relief.

The occupied ceiling layer is at Y90–91 in the gallery and Y80–81 in the two
lower rooms. The upper floors at Y92/Y82 are never touched. Normal standing space,
stairs, retained furniture, inventories, windows, hanging fixtures and all saved
default/tagged BEs are exclusions. Both lodge additions stay at X1211–1215 and
outside the river, moving leaves and machinery. Their actual exterior cells were
read from the verified stopped archive; they were never assumed to be empty.

The complete current saved palace composition includes all four original palace
plans plus the corrected524 depth plan. The bridge baseline is the complete
native-corrected20393-cell composition. Both full composites plus the proposed
layer pass the building3/9 and outdoor9 material checks. State/facing changes are
not material breaks. All93 targets have a retained structural root path.

Twelve small brackets are checked against their actual beam underside. A top slab
above a one-block-high bracket would leave a visible half-block gap; the proposal
uses full joint/end blocks or a bottom slab where that contact is needed. New
perpendicular stair neighbors are refused until explicitly authored/native-reviewed;
the current final composition has none. These geometric checks supplement rather
than replace actual mod-model survival, normal neighbor joining and visual review.

`tools/artistic_secondary_depth.py` reads only the authenticated immutable stopped
checkpoint. It accepts the reconciled owned artistic home, exact source hashes,
actual registry palette and finite archive bounds. Its only writes are new private
plans/manifests, with at most16MiB total output and an explicit two-drive reserve
check. It never launches a runtime, applies a plan, moves a bridge, issues a world
command, spawns an entity, changes an existing source or replays BE/inventory tags.

Each of the five groups has a paired `*-depth.json` and `*-before-VERIFY-ONLY.json`.
The separate `supports-VERIFY-ONLY.json` records unchanged ceiling, facade and
attachment roots. **Before/support files are verification-only, never placement
instructions.** The manifest binds every member, predecessor, source and saved
region hash and includes five proposed room/facade camera angles. Room camera
approaches are checked against actual saved floor/body voxels; model collision
and normal walking are still native review requirements.

The parent must verify the exact current preimages/supports, place each group
once, inspect settled native states without weakening joins, and take upward
room views and close oblique rear-facade photographs. Confirm the kitchen chain,
stair and gallery routes, lodge interiors and existing bridge controls afterward.
Keep the original unchanged views for comparison. Counts, synthetic tests and
source material compliance do not establish artistic or mechanical acceptance.
