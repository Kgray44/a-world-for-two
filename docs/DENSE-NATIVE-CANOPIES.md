# Dense native canopy waves

`tools/artistic_dense_canopies.py` prepares small executable-input groups for real saplings, with separate parent-issued bare-plot readiness. The first current group has twelve irregularly offset roots from the lower meadow through the cliff foot and inland ridge; the two additional source-qualified plots are pink magnolia and pine. The earlier silver-birch location refused nearby saved entities. Nothing is cleared or retried to obtain a preferred tree.

Each tree keeps the sealed R3 ordinary/rare crown, deep-root and eight-block read perimeter. Every cell comes from the exact verified stopped save. Existing trees and block entities in READ-only context are retained; a giant empty ring is unnecessary. New WRITE envelopes preserve other members' full before fields, allowing a wave to run in sequence. Later waves can infill between actual grown canopies after a fresh stopped snapshot rather than stamping duplicate tree shapes or forcing variants.

The exact one-metre NORTH-connected land mask is a mandatory gate, including unbuilt village land. The entire proposed WRITE/crown footprint stays five metres outside it. The mask's independently flooded north/south components are disjoint. Pink magnolia's location has explicit SOUTH_CONNECTED evidence; pine outside that northern study uses the wider measured south-bank authority. Absence from the northern mask alone is never southern planting authority. Compass Bend, eastern22, all existing authored AIR, mechanisms, fountain, woodland routes and western v2 zipline reservations remain protected.

A bounded saved-entity position screen rejects residents, passengers and objects near a proposed plot without exporting their UUIDs, owners or raw NBT. It is not an actual model AABB proof. The unchanged native controller still loads the known field, checks every current block, performs the real live entity query, witnesses its hooks and preserves bee/restoration/quarantine rules before ordinary growth.

`bind_requests` only prepares explicitly reviewed plots from an exact current source receipt and actual installed46 class hashes. It never creates approval, installs files, runs commands or grows a tree. Readiness concerns one bare plot and does not certify whole-world construction or grown-tree appearance. The historical twelve-tree wave reserved at most33 possible native bee slots; together with the two follow-ons and a fifteen-species resident plan, its conservative capacity was54 before reconciling actual bee outcomes. Later waves require a fresh census and budget rather than multiplying this reservation invisibly.

The producer accepts an explicit new verified checkpoint for subsequent waves. It selects up to240 measured candidate roots per finite scan, qualifies at most twelve at a time, and retains failures. Hundreds of eventual canopies remain a rollout objective, not a claim that hundreds have already grown. Normal restart, actual appearance, ecological behavior, whole-bank coverage and resource measurements remain separate real-world evidence.
