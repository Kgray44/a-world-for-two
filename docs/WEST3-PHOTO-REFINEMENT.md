# First native west preview: small refinement

The three new western frontages passed native47 placement, normal joins and a
strict 3,775-cell check. Four actual native screenshots then showed a useful
palette improvement over the rejected striped roofs, together with two concrete
problems: the taller Cooper's north end was too flat and plain, and the Wick's
workbench was too dark. The first street cameras were also too close to a window
frame and pottery display. These observations do not accept the whole town or
authorize wider replication.

`tools.artistic_west_r2_refine` makes one separate 48-cell proposal:

- A genuinely opened, one-metre-projecting upper oriel has a real floor, carved
  supporting knees, timber jambs/head and six fancy panes.
- Four more panes sit recessed in the high gable below the **unchanged** roof,
  with small modeled shoulders creating shadow and depth.
- Two normal vanilla hanging lanterns add practical warm light over the actual
  Wick work area. Existing full floor joists support them above player headroom.
- Narrow supported ledges carry four contained planted pots and a small inert
  pottery vessel. They occupy the frontage pocket, outside the clear walk core.

Every roof cell, existing furniture/model BE, current item stack, stair, door,
two-wide route, retained pottery barrow and zipline reservation remains intact.
The actual post-W3 stopped archive must supply exact sparse before states and
support witnesses. An earlier archive containing no W3 buildings cannot qualify
this delta. Native before verification, sparse placement, normal joins, complete
strict after verification and fresh actual images remain required.

The plant layer is botanical decoration; it does not seed or accept living
residents, insects or wildlife. The two LIFE scopes remain separate. No source
check is presented as an artistic approval, native model-scale measurement or
walkthrough.
