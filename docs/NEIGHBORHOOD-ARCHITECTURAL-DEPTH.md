# A depth layer for the twenty earlier neighborhood buildings

This original pass adds physical relief to Signal Lane's ten buildings, Rose &
Lantern Court's four and Bramble Lane's six. It uses their complete furnished
composites, not the rejected settlement builders or repeated complete facades.
The actual harbor-alley, craft-lane and cottage-hearth screenshots informed the
work: their flat ceiling fields, plain framed panels and heavy existing eaves
need modeled structure without another broad canopy across the narrow streets.

The reviewed proposal changes 1,287 cells: 792 in Signal Lane, 171 in the court
and 324 in Bramble Lane. These are source geometry counts, not native placement,
visual quality or owner acceptance. Six focused tests pass. Every complete
building, including its original floors and roofs, passes the three/nine material
rule; all three complete districts pass the outdoor nine-block cap.

## What changes in three dimensions

Selected wall panels have real half-block-deep **blind** recesses. Paired normal
bottom/top stairs face inward, keeping the continuous solid interior back half
while creating an exterior inset sill and head. They follow the original framed
bays, preserve their posts and avoid glass, fixtures and fixture supports. These
are not new holes through the walls or color changes presented as architecture.

Selected convex storey corners receive carved columns, angled timber knees,
shaped registers or masonry chamfers. Only small single-cell cornice knees and
short supported window brows project outward. They do not restore the retired
oversized upper boxes or fill the sky with new awnings. Nineteen buildings have
an individually chosen brow or integrated window surround; the greenhouse keeps
its protected entrance lintel and receives modeled opaque rafters instead.

Ceiling coffers attach beneath actual retained opaque floor supports. The native
Dawn of Time support slab is a quarter-block cap (Y12–16 pixels); its beam adds
a half-block rib (Y4–12). In these rooms the lower model edge stays above the
preserved two-cell body clearance. The source leaves fixture gaps, stair voids,
vaults and skylights alone instead of inventing a flat ceiling where none exists.
Every greenhouse glass cell remains exact; only original opaque rafter sections
become the narrower native models.

## Admission and evidence limits

`tools.artistic_neighborhood_depth` first emits private artwork geometry and
unbound verification-only complete compositions. Its separate `prepare` function
authenticates those exact frozen artifacts against a verified stopped checkpoint,
reads every proposed actual target from immutable MCA data, checks the expected
identity and supplied properties, and refuses any saved BE at a target, including
default or unwritten contents. It then emits separate before, delta, full
verification and recoverable before-state artifacts for each district.

Only a district's **delta** may be placed after its fresh zero-difference native
before check. Before/full/rollback artifacts carry the construction-forbidden
flag. Deltas contain no commands, inventory payloads, sign rewrites or BE replay.
The complete original source tags, furniture, windows, doors, stairs, routes,
skylights and retained supports remain exact. This is not a comparison of all
untouched live inventory contents; it is a refusal to target or replay them.

The current private binding checks all 1,287 actual saved targets against the
verified stopped checkpoint. Native placement, normal neighbor-shape settling,
strict complete after verification, real screenshots, model-scale inspection and
walking remain separate parent-owned gates. Local source/model checks cannot
establish their success or the owner's artistic approval. Failed receipts and
all earlier artwork versions remain retained.
