# The Captain's Rest: one reference-led harbor hall

This is one original Minecraft composition based closely on the owner's Captain's Head reference by Jamie Read/Rare. It translates the reference's massing, material relationships and details; it does not claim an identical pixel copy. The unchanged owner-supplied image remains private, SHA256 `535bf2c1e639da536d2b16bd52428414f983338f46355fd8e37a55d632dd69da`. No image, texture or downloaded schematic is copied into this design or the repository.

The taller left-hand hall has muted slate/teal infill, heavy dark timber and projecting quoin/corbels. A lower, warm clay-tiled hipped annex carries a broad actual Macaw casement. A deep braced porch, slim carved eyebrows, a projecting nautical crest, genuinely suspended trade sign, restrained potted shrubs, ivy and small warm lamps give the exterior depth. Actual registered Macaw/Dawn roof pieces, Mythrais carvings and modeled furniture supply three-dimensional detail. Raw blue/green plaster textures were inspected and rejected as too saturated for this reference; restrained cyan terracotta gives the intended weathered gray/teal field.

The composition occupies a newly measured northern headland, separate from the accepted western three-frontage prototype. Main hall: X1034–1050/Z−1293–−1281; low annex: X1051–1058/Z−1290–−1281. The porch's structural front ends at Z−1278. Its roof may project another metre, while the two-wide entrance turns left toward X1037–1038. The natural hollow around X1040–1043/Z−1276–−1272 remains. Two gravel cells at X1042–1043/Z−1277 have air immediately below; the porch was shortened specifically to avoid making foundations over them. Every proposed floor column has measured dry gravel at Y63 and non-air/non-water material below, rather than a filled ocean platform.

The ground floor is a furnished social hall, not a bare shell:

- A walk-behind slate-topped bar, pantry barrels, ship models, readable notice, actual manual smoker/crafting/cutting stations and a small shared supper.
- Three distinct table groups, waiting bench and a usable shared cartography table, with logbooks, cups, warm light and exposed shallow ceiling joists.
- A broad-casement supper annex with couch, real seats, separate galley storage, contained wash-water, cabinet and native stove/cooking pot.

The upper floor has two genuine native blue beds and lockers, a carved privacy screen, shared chart/logbook table, a reading couch, a separate conversation bay, a window-side bench and close night lamps. A real two-wide six-step internal stair reaches it. Its opening and ceiling joists respect the complete stair headroom; it does not rely on teleporting. Both floors have explicit two-high walking routes and several smaller furnished groups. Eleven small lamps create local pools of warmth.
