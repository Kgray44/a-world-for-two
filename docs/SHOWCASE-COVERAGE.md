# Showcase coverage

Candidate **0.1.0-candidate.23** has **197 enabled mod JARs**, plus **1 disabled optional mod**. This is an auditable coverage report, not completion or multiplayer acceptance.

The historical composite contains **55,842 mod-block positions** across **30 mod artifacts**. **13** artifacts have supplied items and **0** have summons in construction plans. Of **95** artifacts needing meaningful physical demonstrations, **63** have no mod-owned block/item/summon evidence. Presence in the remaining rows is partial evidence; it does not establish working gameplay.

**5** mods have related real vanilla interaction sites (including crafting tables and furnaces). These are reported separately from mod-owned blocks; their presence does not establish the mod behavior.

The sole scoped functional demonstration recorded here is the Grand River Drawbridge: two native 55-degree open/close cycles with all 11 temporary tickets released. That receipt covers Create and the Control Room bridge integration. Post-restart cycles, rigged-vessel clearance, player traversal and visual review remain separate.

## What the evidence means

- **Placed blocks:** exact archived native checks for ten base plans and five detail passes, overlaid by the settled bridge verification. Last writer wins; overlap is not double-counted.
- **Supplied items:** explicit native item IDs and counts in archived planned inventories. These may have changed during play; their presence is not a completed crafting/delivery/fishing loop.
- **Construction summons:** real entity IDs in verified construction plans. This report does not infer current entity survival, normal AI or sailing from a summon command.
- **Related vanilla stations:** real crafting tables, furnaces or other potential interaction sites are listed for mods using vanilla blocks. Their presence does not verify the mod interaction.
- **Staged:** source plans only. No physical, functional or player acceptance is claimed.
- **Library, client, performance and service features:** an explicit classification gives the appropriate check. Unknown identities/features stay under review.
