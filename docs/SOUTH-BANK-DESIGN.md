# Willow Crown and Bramble Steps

The south-west bank rises from a grassy coast at Y64–68 through a steep hillside
to a natural upper village at Y121–132. The expansion uses that profile to create
a coastal supply street, a stepped craft neighborhood and a quieter garden crown.
It preserves the completed homestead, pirate quarter and drawbridge, including
their planned air clearance, and retains every surveyed existing container at its
exact position. Deep archaeological containers do not prohibit harmless building
work far above them.

The original generator is `tools/settlement_south.py`. It has no server connection,
does not edit world files and never writes the shared `plan.json`. The deployment
owner prepares a current complete backup, runs the generator, reviews its uniquely
named plan/manifest, then admits the exact plan hash through the native builder.
Generation alone is not installation or acceptance.

The native ground-filtered survey is `south-bank-survey-0.6.5.json`, completed with
zero native survey failures. The input is a four-block grid; interpolation fits
small lots and paths between the actual samples. Earthwork remains bounded rather
than flattening the bank. Fine terrain, foliage and aesthetic blending still need
the intended-shader visual pass.

The rooflines, timber/plaster colors, business functions, room layouts and height
changes distinguish the buildings. Common materials keep the district cohesive.
Exterior detail includes recessed modded windows, shutters, small planters, roofed
stoops, integrated chimneys, signs and warm lanterns. Purposeful interiors leave
circulation rather than filling every floor tile with furniture.

The generation uses actual registered blocks from Macaw's roofs/windows/doors/
lights/paths, Handcrafted, Supplementaries, Chipped, Farmer's Delight, Farm & Charm,
Create, Music Maker and Vinery. Exposure equipment is included in the small shared
field kit. The manifest records the exact block/item use and each room inventory;
it does not count a library or background optimization mod as a physical exhibit.
The Create millstone/chute/depot are explicitly an unpowered installation awaiting
native commissioning and recipe routing. No working-mill claim follows from their
placement.

The Bramble Steps promenade connects the shore and upper crown through 28 named
routes, including a connection for every building. Native stone stairs and three-
wide paving provide 2,353 walking columns with three blocks of cleared headroom.
The manifest retains every route node, installed elevation and floor column for
review. An in-memory check against the exact frozen geometry found zero adjacent
grade violations, zero route grade violations and zero headroom obstructions.
The largest actual path fill is six blocks and cut is four. These are source-plan
checks; live placement and traversal have separate acceptance.

The two-chair **Two Wishes Rose Lookout**, centered at 1027 / 131 / −955, is a
small optional discovery with note supplies. Its approach currently follows
natural ground rather than a formal promenade spur. Small grounded planting
pockets and an apple orchard soften the town's edge. A reachable small discovery
chest sits near the pottery walk. The plans add no persistent creatures, command
blocks or permanent chunk-loading tickets.
