# Marigold & Tide garden pilot

This single flower-shop pilot adds a low carved cherry flower ledge beneath the
existing fancy oriel, four small wall-rooted ivy accents, two short berry-lit
eave brackets, a narrow garden trellis and four contained flowerpots. It retains
the shop's furniture, containers, doors, windows, signs and original structure.

The trellis is a supported garden screen, with a small garland attached to real
cherry posts. Each leaf has a literal face-adjacent path to a new vanilla cherry
log. It is not a hand-stamped forest tree. Regions Unexplored and Fish of Thieves
provide the contained flowers; Chipped provides flowering timber, ivy and the
vined screen; Mythrais provides small carved supports. No loose sapling or azalea
is planted.

The source reads the exact clean post-recovery e55 checkpoint. Every new target
must be actual saved AIR. Wall roots, gravel-stack bottoms, existing shop state,
the two-wide side passage and the zipline/public-route reservations are checked
separately. The complete shop plus additions passes the owner's 3/9 material
rule. A first fascia that entered reserved street headroom was refused and
removed from the design; the original fancy-window sill stays intact.

The pinned RU and Fish of Thieves registration bytecode refers to the native
`FlowerPotBlock` family, and Chipped's registration bytecode refers to the native
`VineBlock` family. The recipe records exact JAR and class hashes. Those are local
API provenance witnesses, not proof of concrete native instances, plant survival
or visual acceptance. Proper vine side flags and literal full wall supports are
provided for every segment. Berry pendants have real timber undersides and
short three-block lengths. No block entity data or inventory is copied.

Only `botanical-depth-PROPOSAL.json` may be placed after fresh native admission.
The other files are verification-only. The whole-shop verification checks block
states and deliberately does not claim inventory acceptance. Native support and
survival checks, ordinary neighbor settling, actual walking, day/night shader
views and owner acceptance remain required before repeating this treatment
elsewhere. The retained early refusal receipts remain available.
