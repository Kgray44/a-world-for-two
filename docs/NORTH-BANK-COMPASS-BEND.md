# Compass Bend

Eight fully modeled buildings extend the north-bank village along one crooked,
three-wide lane. Short graded doorsteps, two-wide back passages, planted shop
windows and four small seating pockets connect the houses to Rose Reach. The
existing oak and dark-cherry trees remain in their small courts; the music-shop
approach bends around the cherry trunk. Main footprints and foundations use
actual saved dry land, with no shoreline infill.

| Building | Inhabited rooms | Actual architectural change |
|---|---:|---|
| Seabird Ledger | 2 | Cut-corner customs study, blue loggia and asymmetric roof |
| Three Kestrels | 3 | Unequal window bays and a two-metre set-back top floor with a guarded terrace |
| Brass Finch | 3 | Carved steep gable, projecting instrument window and clock workroom |
| Anchor & Apple | 2 | Recessed produce corner, low half-hip and planted kitchen bay |

| Rosemary Court | 2 | Terrain-fitted stone collar, garden oriel and seed-library household |
| Salt-Wind House | 3 | Cut coastal corner, vertical sea bay and inset lookout bedroom |

The 20 rooms include real modded tables, seating, cupboards, counters, shelves,
trade displays, household beds, desks, contained flowers and compact wall lamps.
Half-height ceiling coffers preserve two metres of circulation headroom. Native
smokers remain unlit until players use them. No furniture from existing buildings
is replaced, and no saved block entity or inventory is reloaded.

`tools/artistic_north_bank_compass_bend.py` produces a separate new placement,
exact saved before state, retained foundation supports, an unchanged Rose Reach
junction proof and a solid-only normal-neighbor-shape artifact. Old junction
pavers are verified, never placed again. Complete-building material 3/9 and
outdoor maximum-nine checks apply to the final furnished geometry.

This is executable construction source, with native placement, strict physical
verification, walking and actual day/night shader review recorded separately by
the parent operator. Source checks do not establish beauty or resident spawning.
Contained botanical decoration is included; broader planting and living animal,
pet, villager and Ribbit populations remain distinct LIFE passes.
