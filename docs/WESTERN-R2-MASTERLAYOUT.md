# Western R2 layout direction

The full western shore must become one continuous, tightly connected pirate
village. The first three native frontages establish a testable style; they do
not fill the land. Fourteen further individually composed masses in
`tools.artistic_west_r2_layout` extend into the northern headlands, western tip
and eastern neck that the rejected extension left open.

The proposed northern hook has a narrow lantern home, a chamfered needleworker,
an opposite tall bookbinder and a bent herbal shop. A narrow rigging house faces
the separate Captain's Head-inspired hall across a small lane. The western quay
uses a cropped sailor home, a shallow net workshop **north of** the actual wet
inlet and a taller carpenter's house. Bent pantries, houses and a small
mender/tea room turn around the neck's real shore notches. These are distinct
plans with varied fronts, widths, depths and heights; there is no uniform lot
grid or periodic bright roof stripe.

Every proposed main outline lies within the exact measured north-connected
locator and excludes the held W3 and hall footprints. Their conservative height
envelopes avoid the exact placed western zipline and its reserved flight cells.
Six crooked lane intentions provide an explicit walking backbone. Two desired
neck shoulders fall outside the measured north land and remain refused; they
must be redesigned or surveyed, never paved into water or silently narrowed.

The locator is historical saved geography. It does not prove current native
FULL body space, dry structural support, slope/steps, model headroom or inventory
custody. In particular, retained-object columns are not natural building ground.
No route in this massing file is a qualified native walking or placement plan.

The layout is deliberately incomplete. Existing western stock anchors still
need inventory-safe redesigned envelopes; more small shore buildings and
purposeful courts must close the residual gaps. A plot count cannot demonstrate
that the whole north bank is filled. Full geometry follows actual W3 refinement
and visual review, then exact current before/support admission, ordinary native
joins, complete verification and real street/interior views. No demolition,
replication, inventory transfer or resident spawning is authorized by this file.
