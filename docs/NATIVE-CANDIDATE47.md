  normal neighbor rules and verified. Actual screenshots exposed a plain upper

  the original prototype is not silently rewritten or propagated.

  check found exactly two roof-end connection differences. Pinned native roof
  code confirms those end caps; a separate verification-only derivation preserves
  the source and failed receipt. Actual views and subsequent checks remain open.
- The imported fisherman’s shop received the first floor/loft/stair phase. The
  furnished second phase waits for the actual first-phase room/stair review and
  its own current preimage check. Existing exterior, doors and storage remain.

  its two retained glue volumes. The complete prospective bridge passed native
  geometry verification. Its powered cycle then failed: both existing receivers
  were absent from the native radio network. Transmitter input,55degree programs

  tickets. A separately reviewed normal-lifecycle repair is still a proposal;
  no new successful cycle or restart is claimed.
- The R5 fountain passed a real pumped-header fill, four flowing cascades,
  physical STOP/lower-jet drainage and reopening. The header was not seeded and
  no kinetic NBT/RPM was injected. A full header normally backpressures the pump;
  this does not claim continuous circulation. Restart persistence and final
  artistic/player-use acceptance remain separate.
- Eight additional native sapling outcomes succeeded with resource cleanup.
  Changed preimages were held. A golden-larch attempt restored its two owned
  sapling/grass cells but retained two untracked water/ice differences outside
  its write domain. Its quarantine and all failure records remain held for exact
  review; no ambient water/ice rollback or automatic planting retry occurred.
  This is not completion of the whole dense south-bank forest.

The eight successful new growth audits report no new nests, stored bee slots or
live entity UUIDs from those attempts. That does not mean the world contains no
bees. No curated living LIFE cohort has been spawned.

Twelve actual native framebuffer views were captured for the first frontage,
interiors, empty imported shop, bridge and flowing fountain. Their observed

multiplayer benchmark. These views support further refinement, not final quality
