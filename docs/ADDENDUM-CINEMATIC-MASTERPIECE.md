## 1. EXTREME DETAIL: EVERYWHERE

This is the highest creative priority.

Avoid simple rectangular buildings, blank exterior walls, oversized empty rooms, repetitive pathways, and structures that look procedurally assembled.

Every important building should include thoughtful architectural detailing.

### Exterior details

Use appropriate combinations of:

- Varied rooflines and roof heights
- Roof overhangs, dormers, chimneys, and gables
- Architectural depth and recessed windows
- Window boxes with flowers
- Decorative shutters
- Wooden beams and support framing
- Balconies, porches, and terraces
- Arches, pillars, and railings
- Stone foundations and retaining walls
- Carefully varied wall textures
- Climbing vines and vegetation
- Flowering bushes
- Hanging lanterns
- Garden fences and gates
- Covered entrances
- Outdoor furniture
- Flower pots, baskets, barrels, and crates
- Appropriately weathered or aged surfaces
- Small individual architectural accents

Use Macaw's collection, Mythrais, Handcrafted, Supplementaries, FramedBlocks, Rechiseled, Chipped, and other installed decorative mods extensively.

Avoid random block variation that makes buildings look noisy. Create coherent material palettes with intentional accents.

### Interior details

Make every important room feel inhabited.

Include:

- Furniture arranged naturally
- Rugs and varied flooring
- Curtains or window decorations where available
- Tables with appropriate objects
- Bookshelves and reading nooks
- Paintings and photographs
- Candles, lanterns, and warm lamps
- Flower vases
- Kitchen ingredients and utensils
- Comfortable seating arrangements
- Storage furniture
- Fireplace decorations
- Decorative ceiling beams
- Bedside tables
- Blankets and pillows
- Shelving, cabinets, and wall ornaments
- Appropriate little personal belongings

Rooms should have clear purposes and believable layouts.

Avoid enormous empty interior spaces and furniture randomly scattered against walls.

**The cottage should look like somewhere KG and Sera would genuinely want to live.**
